# GTM Plan (30-day, $0 paid)

The exact ICP is the technical founder or founding CTO at a B2B SaaS company with 5-40 employees, post-MVP, pre-Series A, spending $1.5k-$20k/month on AI tooling across coding assistants, chat copilots, and model APIs. Secondary ICP is an engineering manager at 50-200 person startups where procurement is still founder-led and spend ownership is fragmented.

Trigger moments are highly specific: (1) right after monthly cloud/finance review when AI tool line items look messy, (2) right before annual renewal of coding copilots, (3) after a budget freeze memo, (4) after hiring a finance lead who asks for spend accountability, and (5) after team complaints about duplicate tooling. What they search or scroll before intent: “Cursor vs Copilot cost,” “Claude team pricing,” “reduce AI API bill,” “engineering tooling budget template,” or “SaaS cost optimization for startups.”

Where they already hang out: r/SaaS, r/startups, r/Entrepreneur, Indie Hackers, YC Bookface (if available), RevGenius Slack, specific founder pods on X, and operator Discord groups where devtool choices are discussed. The distribution move is not generic posting; it is thread-based teardown content: “We audited 15 seed startups and found median 28% AI spend waste — here are 5 avoidable mistakes.” Each post links to a constrained one-page audit flow.

First 100 users in 30 days:
1. DM 60 founder/CTO profiles with personalized one-line cost benchmark; ask for 7-minute walkthrough.
2. Offer free “AI Spend Snapshot” in 3 founder communities with strict cap (first 25 teams).
3. Publish 5 comparison pages (Cursor vs Copilot, Claude Team vs ChatGPT Business, etc.) with transparent pricing citations.
4. Partner with 3 startup CFO fractional operators to run Planit for their portfolio.
5. Add a share artifact (“We cut 31% AI spend with Planit”) to drive peer referrals.

Unfair channel for Credex: convert audit insights directly into financing/offers matched to savings potential and renewal timing. Competitors can provide advice; Credex can package capital + optimization signal.

Week-1 traction target if this works: 300 unique visitors, 80 completed audits (27% CVR), 28 leads (35% lead capture on completers), 8 qualified consult calls, and at least 2 pilot financing conversations.
