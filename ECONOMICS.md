# Unit Economics Model

Assumption set (conservative): an average converted Credex lead results in $12,000 annualized gross revenue contribution when financing is activated (blended margin-adjusted value across small and mid tickets). If contribution margin is 35%, gross contribution per converted customer is about $4,200/year.

Lead value model:
- Audit completed -> consultation booked: 12%
- Consultation booked -> credit purchase: 25%
- Effective conversion from audit complete -> purchase: 3%
- Expected value per audit completion: $4,200 x 0.03 = $126

If lead capture rate from completed audits is 35%, expected value per captured lead is roughly $126 / 0.35 = $360. This gives an upper bound CAC target per qualified lead below $120 for healthy payback and below $200 if strategic.

Channel CAC estimates from GTM (mostly founder-time costed):
- Direct founder outreach: $40-$90 per captured lead equivalent (high effort, high intent)
- Community posts and teardown threads: $10-$35 per captured lead equivalent
- Partner operator channel: $60-$140 per captured lead equivalent but stronger close rates
- SEO comparison pages (after setup): $5-$20 marginal CAC equivalent

Profitability threshold scenario:
- If 100 audits complete/month
- 35 leads captured
- 12% book consultation = 4.2 consults
- 25% consult-to-purchase = ~1 new customer/month
- 1 customer x $4,200 annual gross contribution = $4,200 yearly contribution from that cohort

At this level, the tool is already economically positive if fixed monthly operating cost (hosting + AI + maintenance) remains under roughly $350-$600 and founder labor is leveraged across compounding channels.

$1M ARR path in 18 months (rough but actionable):
- Target 240 net new revenue customers with ~$4,200 average annual contribution each = ~$1,008,000
- Required monthly pace by month 18: ~14 net new/month average across ramp
- Back-solve funnel using 3% audit->purchase conversion: need ~467 audits/month by late-stage run rate
- With 35% lead capture, that is ~163 leads/month and ~20 consults/month at 12% consult booking rate

What must be true:
1. Audit completion-to-lead remains >=30%.
2. Consult booking from leads stays >=10% with quality routing.
3. Sales close from consult stays >=20%.
4. CAC per captured lead stays below ~$120 blended.
5. Product trust is high enough that users share results publicly, lowering acquisition cost over time.
